The map

You do not run six chatbots. You run one Manager. Desks are who you talk to. Specialists are who he talks to. Mail agents do not chat — they match a job and ding the tech.

You
→ Ask PermitAIO (purple button)
→ Main · Permit · HOA · Support
→ Manager asks Investigator + Form Specialist when needed
→ Permit mail / HOA mail ding the right tech

He only answers from facts on file: jobs, HOA, Libraries, correction lessons, research reports. If it is not there, he says so. He does not change status by himself.

How you teach him

1. Libraries

New form, NOA, building-dept note, HOA contact. Put it in Libraries. Don’t paste it into chat.

2. Research report

Ask PermitAIO → Feed a research report. Paste. Pick who wrote it. County / city. Load into him.

3. Correction lesson

Open the job → Feed a correction. Paste what they asked. Attach the letter. Optional: what cleared it.

4. Change a rule

Tell Manager in this Grok chat, or write it on Settings → Grok Manager.
