Fenestration Energy Code Check

Check U-factor & SHGC against 2023 Florida Building Code — Energy Conservation (8th Edition)
Screening tool only — not a permit submittal. This checks a single window/door's NFRC-labeled U-factor and SHGC against the Florida prescriptive energy path (R402.1.2). Whole-building compliance can also be shown via the Simulated Performance (R405), UA-alternative (R402.1.5), or ERI (R406) paths — those aren't covered here. Also confirm against the project's approved Form R405 or Energy Performance Level Display Card before install.
Project Inputs
Florida is split into just two zones under the FBC. All HVHZ counties are Zone 1.
Replacements and additions generally must meet the same fenestration limits when replacing the entire product. Sash-only repairs are typically exempt.
Impact-rated products may use a relaxed U-factor limit in Zone 2 (footnote j, R402.1.2).
NFRC Label Values
Lower is better. Typical impact vinyl: 0.28–0.40. Aluminum impact: often 0.55–0.70.
Lower is better in Florida (blocks more solar heat). Range 0–1. Typical impact tinted: 0.20–0.30.
Skylights have separate, looser limits under FBC-EC.
