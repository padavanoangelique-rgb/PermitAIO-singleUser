How to read an NOA number

Format: NOA numbers look like YY-MMDD.NN — year, month/day of approval, sequence. Example: 22-0315.02.

Florida Product Approval numbers look like FL#####-R# — the R# is the revision. Always specify the current revision on your permit application.

HVHZ (High Velocity Hurricane Zone) = Miami-Dade and Broward counties. HVHZ approval is required for any glazing installed in those counties. Palm Beach and Wind-Borne Debris Regions use Florida Product Approval only, unless the product is HVHZ-listed.
